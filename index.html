Capital budgeting

Evaluate a project using discounted cash-flow techniques.

NPV · IRR · PI · PAYBACK

Project assumptions

Enter assumptions and evaluate the project.

How to interpret the results

• NPV: value added today after covering the initial investment.

• IRR: discount rate that makes project NPV equal zero.

• PI: present value of future inflows per dollar invested.

• Payback: time needed to recover initial cash outlay; simple payback ignores time value of money.

For independent projects with conventional cash flows, positive NPV is generally the strongest value-creation rule. IRR and PI are useful supporting metrics.

Weighted average cost of capital

WACC = E/V × Ke + D/V × Kd × (1 − Tax rate).

Financial statement analysis

Diagnose liquidity, leverage and profitability using sample company data.

RATIO ANALYSIS

Northstar Ltd. · Input financial data

Calculate ratios to interpret the company's financial position.

Ratio categories

Liquidity — ability to meet short-term obligations.

Leverage — reliance on debt financing and asset coverage.

Profitability — earnings generated from sales, assets and equity.

Analyst reminder

A ratio is not a conclusion. Compare it with prior periods, industry peers, accounting policies and the company's operating model. Use average assets and average equity for ROA and ROE when the data is available.
