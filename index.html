Welcome to FINANCEVERSE! This is an interactive Financial Learning & Business Simulation Game. Here, you will act as an Investor, Financial Analyst and CFO, make financial decisions, and learn Finance through realistic business-style scenarios.

A Project by Samiur Rahman Sami – BRAC Business School Student

1. Main Objective of the Game

The main objectives are to:

  • Learn about Investment and the Stock Market.
  • Develop the ability to make sound Financial Decisions.
  • Practice different Corporate Finance calculations.
  • Create and manage an Investment Portfolio.
  • Make business financial decisions like a CFO.
  • Improve your Game Level by earning XP.

2. Game Modules & Activities

01 · Investment Market

Explore fictional companies, analyze performance, choose shares and practice virtual trading.

02 · Portfolio Management

Build a portfolio, monitor investment value, analyze profit/loss and consider risk.

03 · Corporate Finance

Practice NPV, IRR, PI, Payback Period and WACC calculations.

04 · DCF Valuation

Analyze future cash flows, apply a discount rate and estimate intrinsic value.

05 · CFO Simulator

Make financing decisions, compare debt and equity, and improve financial performance.

06 · Financial Ratio Analysis

Analyze Liquidity, Profitability, Leverage and Efficiency Ratios.

3. XP, Levels & Mission System

  • XP (Experience Points): Earn experience through game activities.
  • Levels: Track your progress and development.
  • Missions: Complete specific Financial Learning Activities.
  • Activity Tracking: Review your in-game activities.
Specific XP awards, level thresholds and mission scoring may vary by the current demo build and are not guaranteed for every activity.

4. How to Play the Demo

  1. Enter the game using the Demo Workspace.

  6. Track your XP, Level, Missions and Activity.

5. Important Game Rules

  1. This is an Educational Simulation Game, not a real Stock Exchange.
  2. No Real Money Investment is required.
  3. All virtual trading must use fictional/virtual capital.
  4. Use the correct Financial Formulas for calculations.
  5. Prioritize Financial Analysis over guesswork.
  6. Consider potential Risk before every Investment Decision.
  7. Monitor your Learning Progress regularly.

FINANCEVERSE · CORPORATE VALUATION LAB

Intrinsic Valuation & Investment Decision Centre

Build a five-year unlevered discounted cash flow (DCF) model for any listed simulator company. Change the assumptions, estimate enterprise and equity value, test a margin of safety and inspect valuation sensitivity.

Model basis: five-year unlevered FCFF DCF. FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − capital expenditure − change in net working capital. Enterprise value is discounted operating cash flows plus discounted terminal value. Equity value = enterprise value − total debt + cash. Intrinsic value per share = equity value ÷ shares outstanding. This simplified teaching model assumes stable operating margins and financing structure.

How to interpret the results

• NPV: value added today after covering the initial investment.

• IRR: discount rate that makes project NPV equal zero.

• PI: present value of future inflows per dollar invested.

• Payback: time needed to recover initial cash outlay; simple payback ignores time value of money.

For independent projects with conventional cash flows, positive NPV is generally the strongest value-creation rule. IRR and PI are useful supporting metrics.

Weighted average cost of capital

WACC = E/V × Ke + D/V × Kd × (1 − Tax rate).
